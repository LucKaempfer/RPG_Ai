Hello and welcome to my first ever ,,real,, Game!
This is a Rougelike Dungeonbattler but limited to its Basics.
You will need to type in the commands you want to take.
The Game consists of your Base and the Dungeon.

You Start at Level one with 100 health points,
5 Attackpoints and 5 Defencepoints

Your Actions will reward you with XP so you can level up.
Every levelup you can decide to add to your health, attack or defence.

During the Runs you will also gather Bloodcrystals.
These can be used to buy you permanent Upgrades to the Game.

During a run you will face a choice of rooms.
In these Rooms there can be monsters or loot or both.
